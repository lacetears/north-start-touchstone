Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: make dependable, handmade food for the people who live and work nearby. We want the shop to feel familiar, welcoming, and easy to visit.

How We Source

We focus on straightforward ingredients and work with local and regional suppliers when practical. Small-batch baking also helps us keep the daily selection fresh.

Meet the Team

Our bakers start early, our counter team knows the regulars, and everyone helps keep the bakery moving during the morning rush.
